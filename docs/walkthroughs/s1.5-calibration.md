# S1.5 — Calibration layer walkthrough

Preflight: see [README](README.md). Best after the S1 walkthrough (claims +
imported repos give the quiz something to corroborate).

## Take a quiz

- [ ] **Do:** open Skills; pick a canonical skill (e.g. one you actually know);
      start the calibration quiz. (First take on a skill generates its question
      banks — expect a brief wait state.)
      **Expect:** rounds of 6 multiple-choice questions; answering well moves you up
      a difficulty band, poorly moves you down; the session settles within ≤18
      questions (~5–10 min).
- [ ] **Do:** finish and read the result screen.
      **Expect:** your settled band AND the exact points delta written — plain
      honesty about what just happened to your evidence.
- [ ] **Do:** immediately try to retake the same skill's quiz.
      **Expect:** a cooldown message with the next-retake date (7 days per skill).

## Claims convert to evidence

- [ ] **Do:** find a skill you hold as an unvalidated claim (resume-sourced);
      take its quiz.
      **Expect:** the claim chip's quiz CTA was there before you started; after a
      good result the skill now has evidenced points (the claim itself never counted).

## The L3 corroboration cap

- [ ] **Do:** on a skill whose ONLY evidence is imported repos (no quiz, no cert),
      check its level on Skills/Profile.
      **Expect:** even with many repo points, the level shows at most L3 — with an
      explanation pointing at the corroboration rule. Add quiz evidence for that
      skill (after cooldown) and the cap lifts.

## Certificates

- [ ] **Do:** submit a certificate from an allowlisted provider (Coursera, edX,
      Udacity, freeCodeCamp, …) with its public verify URL.
      **Expect:** GLaDOS fetches the public page, softly checks the name (mismatch =
      warning, never a block), asks you to CONFIRM the course→skill mapping, then
      writes verified certificate evidence dated to the issue date.
- [ ] **Do:** submit a certificate from a provider NOT on the list (any URL).
      **Expect:** stored as a plain claim with a one-line quiz nudge — no crash, no
      gate.

## Commit→post

- [ ] **Do:** open a GitHub-linked project with ≥10 of your own pushed commits
      since the last draft (fresh imports count from import).
      **Expect:** the post surface offers to draft; drafts are grounded in the real
      commit range (and your LinkedIn-review advice if present); drafting is capped
      at 2/week; the events page shows the `post_drafted` anchor — the counter reset.

## Trust teaching

- [ ] **Do:** open "How levels work".
      **Expect:** the ladder (claims → corroboration → evidence → levels) rendered
      with YOUR profile as the worked example, quizzes and certificates as the rungs.

## Admin calibration console

- [ ] **Do:** open Admin → evidence/calibration sections.
      **Expect:** quiz telemetry readouts that honestly say "not enough data" until
      their minimum samples are met (a brand-new install shows mostly these states —
      correct behavior, not a bug); the evidence-vs-quiz divergence slot now renders
      real rows once data exists; the what-if replay (flag-gated) lets you change
      point weights, replay history, and see the level diff BEFORE applying.

## Negative delta (hard to self-test — covered by tests)

- [ ] **Note:** scoring WORSE on a retake 7+ days later is the only user-visible
      negative delta, and it is bounded to quiz credit. The fixed-clock tests in
      `tests/evidence-core.service.test.ts` and `tests/quiz-sessions.service.test.ts`
      are the worked examples for this math — reading them counts as the walk.
